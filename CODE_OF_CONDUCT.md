# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds, languages and experience. Discuss technical decisions with evidence and focus criticism on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, spam and disclosure of someone else's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary, with an explanation where practical.

Report concerns privately to hanwei5512@126.com. Reports will be handled with discretion. These expectations apply to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other official community spaces.
